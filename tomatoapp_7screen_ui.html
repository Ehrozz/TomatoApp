Symptoms
Dark brown to black lesions with concentric rings appear on older, lower leaves first. Lesions are surrounded by yellow halos and may coalesce as the disease progresses.
Cause
Caused by the fungus Alternaria solani. Spreads rapidly in warm, humid conditions between 24–29°C. Favored by wet foliage and poor air circulation.
Treatment
Apply copper-based or chlorothalonil fungicides at first sign. Remove infected leaves promptly. Repeat every 7–10 days.
